# Salon Owner Dashboard: Analytics & Reporting Analysis

Based on my 20 years of experience building enterprise-grade SaaS platforms and specifically looking at the needs of a multi-tenant salon management system, here is a comprehensive analysis of what **Charts** and **Stat Cards** would provide the most immediate business value for a Salon Owner across your navigation links.

A great dashboard doesn't just show data; it highlights actionable insights—things the owner can improve *today*.

---

## 1. Dashboard (Main Overview)
The main dashboard should act as a command center. It needs high-level health indicators.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Daily Occupancy Rate (%):** (Booked hours / Total available hours) - Critical for knowing if chairs are empty.
- **Average Ticket Size:** Average spend per client today vs. last week.
- **Walk-ins vs. Pre-booked:** Ratio of spontaneous clients to scheduled ones.
- **Total Revenue (Today):** Split by Services vs. Retail Products.

**Charts:**
- **Peak Hours Heatmap (Weekly):** A grid showing the busiest days and hours. *Actionable: Tells the owner when to schedule more staff.*
- **Service vs. Product Revenue (Donut Chart):** Shows the revenue split. High-performing salons aim for 20-30% retail sales.

---

## 2. Appointments
Focus on scheduling efficiency, no-shows, and booking trends.

**Stat Cards:**
- **No-Show / Cancellation Rate:** Track lost revenue.
- **Upcoming Appointments (Next 7 Days):** Total count and projected revenue.
- **Rebooking Rate:** Percentage of clients who booked their next appointment before leaving.

**Charts:**
- **Booking Channels (Bar Chart):** Where are appointments coming from? (Phone, Website, Social Media, Walk-in).
- **Cancellations by Reason/Time (Line Chart):** Track when people cancel to enforce cancellation policies effectively.

---

## 3. Clients
Focus on customer lifetime value (CLV), retention, and demographics.

**Stat Cards:**
- **New vs. Returning Clients:** Shows if marketing is working (new) and if service is good (returning).
- **Average Client Lifespan:** How many months/years a client stays active.
- **Client Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Marketing spend divided by new clients.

**Charts:**
- **Client Retention Cohorts (Stacked Area Chart):** Shows how many clients acquired in January are still visiting in June.
- **Top Spenders / VIPs (List/Table):** Top 10 clients by revenue. *Actionable: Target them with loyalty rewards.*

---

## 4. Team (Employees & Shifts)
Focus on staff productivity, commission tracking, and payroll efficiency.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Revenue per Employee (RPE):** The ultimate productivity metric.
- **Staff Utilization Rate:** Time spent with clients vs. idle time.
- **Total Commissions Pending:** Real-time tracking of what is owed to staff.

**Charts:**
- **Top Performers by Revenue & Services (Horizontal Bar Chart):** Leaderboard for the week/month.
- **Labor Cost vs. Revenue (Line Chart):** Ensure payroll doesn't exceed 40-50% of revenue.

---

## 5. Service & Categories
Identify what sells and what takes up too much time.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Most Popular Service:** By sheer volume.
- **Most Profitable Service:** Highest margin (Price - Cost of products - Labor time).
- **Average Service Duration:** Actual vs. Planned time.

**Charts:**
- **Revenue by Service Category (Pie/Donut Chart):** Hair vs. Nails vs. Spa, etc.
- **Service Trends Over Time (Line Chart):** E.g., Highlights peak in summer, Keratin peaks in winter.

---

## 6. Inventory (Products & Stock)
Focus on retail sales, shrinkage, and reorder alerts.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Low Stock Alerts:** Number of items falling below par levels.
- **Total Inventory Value:** Cash tied up on the shelves.
- **Retail Sell-Through Rate:** How fast products are moving.

**Charts:**
- **Top Selling Retail Products (Bar Chart):** See which brands/items drive retail revenue.
- **Dead Stock / Slow Movers (List):** Products that haven't sold in 90+ days. *Actionable: Run a discount campaign.*

---

## 7. Financial & Budgeting
Deep dive into cash flow, profitability, and expense tracking.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Net Profit Margin:** The absolute most important number for the owner.
- **Current Cash Flow:** Cash in vs. Cash out for the month.
- **Outstanding Gift Card Liability:** Total unredeemed gift card value.

**Charts:**
- **Expense Breakdown (Donut Chart):** Rent vs. Payroll vs. Supplies vs. Marketing.
- **Revenue vs. Expenses (Overlaid Line Chart):** Month-over-month profitability tracking.
- **Payment Methods Distribution (Pie Chart):** Cash vs. Credit vs. Online.

---

## 8. Social Media & Marketing
Tie social effort directly to business results.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Engagement to Booking Conversion:** How many social media clicks turned into appointments.
- **Promo Code Usage:** Tracking specific marketing campaigns.
- **Total Audience Growth:** New followers across platforms.

**Charts:**
- **Traffic Sources (Funnel Chart):** Impressions → Profile Views → Link Clicks → Appointments Booked.
- **Post Performance by Time of Day (Scatter Plot):** When is the best time to post for maximum engagement?

## 9. Statistics
Focus on comparative performance and historical data.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth:** Current revenue vs. same period last year.
- **Top Employee (Month):** By total revenue or client retention.
- **Client Satisfaction Score:** Average rating from post-appointment surveys.

**Charts:**
- **Performance by Category (Radar Chart):** Comparing multiple locations or employees across metrics (speed, upsell, retention).
- **Client Status Flow (Sankey Diagram):** Showing how many clients go from New -> Returning -> VIP or New -> Churned.

---

## 10. Waivers
Focus on compliance and risk management.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Unsigned Waivers:** Number of upcoming appointments missing a required waiver.
- **Waivers Completed Today:** Tracking front-desk compliance flow.
- **Total Expired Waivers:** Historical waivers that need client renewal.

**Charts:**
- **Waiver Compliance Rate (Gauge Chart):** Percentage of active clients with valid waivers on file.

---

## 11. Files
Focus on storage utilization and client documentation.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Storage Used:** E.g., "45% of 10GB Used."
- **Recent Uploads:** Number of files uploaded this week.
- **Orphaned Files:** Files not linked to any specific client or appointment.

**Charts:**
- **Storage Breakdown by Type (Donut Chart):** Client Photos vs. PDF Documents vs. Videos.

---

## 12. Roles
Focus on system security and access control.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Total Active Users:** Employees with active dashboard login access.
- **Admin/Manager Count:** Tracking who has high-level permissions.

**Charts:**
- **System Activity by Role (Bar Chart):** System usage and actions categorized by user role (e.g., Receptionists vs. Stylists).

---

## 13. Support
Focus on platform reliability and help requests.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Open Tickets:** Number of unresolved support issues.
- **Average Resolution Time:** How fast issues get fixed.
- **System Uptime:** Platform health indicator.

**Charts:**
- **Tickets by Category (Pie Chart):** Billing vs. Technical vs. Feature Requests.

---

## 14. Settings
Focus on configuration and account health.

**Stat Cards:**
- **Profile Completeness:** "Your salon profile is 80% complete."
- **Active Integrations:** Number of connected external apps (e.g., Stripe, Mailchimp, QuickBooks).

**Charts:**
- *(Settings are not typically chart-heavy, but visual Progress Bars for setup and onboarding steps are highly recommended to encourage full platform utilization).*

---

> [!TIP]
> **Implementation Advice**
> Don't build all of these at once! Start by adding **Occupancy Rate**, **Average Ticket Size**, and **Staff Utilization** to the main dashboard. These three metrics alone will give a Salon Owner the levers they need to drastically increase profitability.
